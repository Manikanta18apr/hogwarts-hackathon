## **📌 Overview**

**DeshaDarsana** is an AI-powered platform designed to help users discover **authentic local experiences** such as:
- Food & street cuisine  
- Cultural spots  
- Hidden gems  
- Local events  
- Activities & workshops  
- Micro-itineraries  

The system works like a **Smart AI Local Guide**, delivering personalized, real-time recommendations based on:
- User interests  
- Location  
- Time of day  
- Budget  
- Mood  
- Popularity + reviews  
- Event availability  

---

## **✨ Features**

### **1. 🤖 AI Chatbot Local Guide**
Ask natural questions like:
- “Best street food near me?”
- “Where can I go for sunset today?”
- “Any cultural events tonight?”

AI responds instantly with curated experiences.

### **2. 🎯 Personalized Recommendations**
AI tailors results using:
- Behavior tracking  
- User preferences  
- Context (time, location, weather)

### **3. 🗺️ Hidden Gems Finder**
Discover lesser-known places ignored by generic travel apps.

### **4. 🎉 Event & Activity Discovery**
Shows:
- Workshops  
- Cultural festivals  
- Meetups  
- Food events  
- Student-friendly activities  

### **5. 🗓️ Smart Mini-Itinerary Generator**
Creates optimized plans for:
- 1–3 hours  
- Half-day  
- Full-day  

### **6. 🌍 Community Reviews & Contributions**
Users can:
- Add new places  
- Share reviews  
- Upload images  
- Recommend experiences
